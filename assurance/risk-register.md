# Risk register

Only `CONTRACT_CONSTRAINED_RISK` findings (`process/finding-taxonomy.md` class 8) belong here: real
risks whose fix would change reviewed contract semantics while the contract is not wrong. Each entry
records the risk, why remediation changes contract semantics, and the future decision path.
`REQUIREMENT_UNCERTAIN`, `CONTRACT_ASSURANCE_DEFECT` and implementation defects are never risk debt.

*No entries (WP-00).*
