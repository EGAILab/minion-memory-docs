Flashcard App – Requirement 03
Learning Evidence, Historical Interpretation & Diagnostics
Mneme Foundation v2.3 / MVP Contract v1.2 aligned — Learning Profile

| Core rule: Persist what happened most durably; preserve what evaluators and evidence mappers concluded at the time; treat current learner state and diagnostics as replaceable interpretations. |
|---|


## 1. Purpose
This requirement defines how the Learning Profile records learner interactions, preserves historical meaning, produces memory-relevant evidence, and derives diagnostics without conflating observations with learner-state truth. It is intentionally richer than Agent Memory capture semantics and must not be generalized away merely to share code.
- Preserve learner history as durable, auditable data.
- Keep RawObservation, EvaluationOutcome, MemoryEvidence, and LearnerState semantically separate.
- Support later reinterpretation as evaluators, evidence mappings, and memory models improve.
- Capture enough context for future analysis without requiring the MVP to implement a research platform.
- Maintain current lightweight front/back review UX.

## 4. LearningEpisode
A LearningEpisode is the bounded container for one coherent learner activity. In the Flashcard MVP it normally contains one primary recall attempt plus reveal/rating-related events. The foundation allows richer multi-event episodes later.
learning_episode:
  id: "lep_..."
  schema_version: 1
  knowledge_unit_id: "ku_..."
  recall_form_id: "rf_..."
  recall_form_version: 3
  started_at: "..."
  completed_at: "..."
  episode_type: review
  context: {...}
- Episode identity is stable and unique.
- Episode context records actual execution context, not authored RecallForm semantics.
- The app does not assume a single global total order across all devices/episodes.
- Within an episode, event order must be deterministic.

## 5. LearningEvent
LearningEvents form the ordered historical timeline inside an episode. The event subtype vocabulary is extensible; the MVP implements only what is needed for the current review flow.
LearningEpisode
  1 PromptPresentedEvent
  2 AttemptEvent
  3 RevealEvent / ExposureEvent
  4 RatingEvent
  5 CompletionEvent
- Each event has an episode-local sequence number independent of wall-clock time.
- occurred_at and recorded_at are conceptually distinct to support offline-first capture and future sync.
- A missing answer or abandoned episode must not automatically become negative memory evidence.
- MVP may serialize several event semantics in one append-only record, provided the domain distinctions remain explicit.

## 6. RawObservation
RawObservation records what the learner actually did or what the application directly observed. It has the strongest historical authority in the evidence pipeline.
raw_observation:
  response_mode: self_assessed_recall
  learner_response: null
  rating: hard
  response_time_ms: 6200
  hints_requested: []
  exposure:
    - target: kp_spelling
      source: answer_reveal

### 6.1 Observation principles
- Store raw learner responses when practical and useful for future reinterpretation.
- Response time is an observation, not a direct memory-strength measurement.
- Hint requests/presentation and exposure are observations, not automatic penalties or successful reviews.
- Unknown or absent information remains unknown; it is never defaulted to false, zero, or failure.
- Data minimization applies: preserve the richest useful observation subject to privacy, retention, and storage boundaries.

## 7. EvaluationOutcome
EvaluationOutcome records what a specific evaluator concluded from an observation. It is distinct from both the raw response and memory attribution.
evaluation_outcome:
  evaluator:
    id: normalized_text_match
    version: 2
  outcome:
    match: false
    normalized_submitted: "accomodate"
    normalized_expected: "accommodate"
    edit_distance: 1
  produced_at: "..."
- Self assessment may produce a minimal historical outcome from Again/Hard/Good/Easy.
- Automatic evaluators may produce richer structured outcomes.
- Optional AI evaluators must identify model/service/version provenance where available.
- Changing an evaluator later does not mutate historical outcomes. A new evaluation may be derived from the same raw observation.

## 8. MemoryEvidence
MemoryEvidence is a versioned interpretation that an observation/outcome supports, contradicts, or is insufficient to assess a memory-relevant target under a particular mapping.
memory_evidence:
  target:
    knowledge_component_id: "kp_spelling"
    knowledge_component_version: 2
  direction: contradicts
  quality: high
  attribution_confidence: high
  source_outcome_id: "eo_..."
  mapping:
    id: "emap_..."
    version: 1

### 8.1 Evidence constraints
- RecallForm target declarations describe potential evidence, not evidence actually obtained.
- A failed multi-target task must not automatically mark all declared targets as failed.
- Evidence may be positive, negative/contradictory, insufficient, or ambiguous.
- Evidence quality may reflect response mode, cue/hint assistance, evaluator reliability, or ambiguity, but these interpretations are mapping/model concerns rather than raw facts.
- Historical evidence retains mapping/provenance/version information and is not rewritten when mappings improve.

## 9. Retrieval versus exposure
The MVP must distinguish active retrieval from passive exposure because showing an answer or related context can affect later performance without proving successful recall.
AttemptEvent
  retrieved_target: kp_meaning
  result: successful self-rated recall

ExposureEvent
  exposed_targets:
    - kp_spelling
    - kp_example_usage

| Rule: Exposure can inform future models, but it is never recorded as successful retrieval merely because content was displayed. |
|---|


## 10. Hint progression and assistance
Hints are authored/support artifacts; hint usage is learner history. The event model should preserve the sequence of assistance when available.
attempt 1: unaided → failed
hint 1: low-strength cue
attempt 2: failed
hint 2: stronger cue
attempt 3: successful
The Flashcard MVP may only record whether a hint was used, but the schema must not assume that a single hint_count is the final semantic model.

## 11. Historical execution snapshot
Each completed episode must preserve enough immutable historical semantics to explain what the learner actually encountered after Knowledge artifacts evolve.
- RecallForm ID + version.
- Knowledge target IDs + versions relevant to the activity.
- Prompt/stimulus actually shown when needed for interpretation.
- Expected response/evaluation definition or evaluator reference/version.
- EvidenceMapping reference/version used historically.
- Hints actually available/used when relevant.
- Asset ID/version/checksum references for non-inline media.
- Execution context needed to interpret the observation.
Snapshots follow semantic sufficiency, not complete duplication. Large media, code environments, or external assets should be referenced by stable version/checksum when practical rather than copied into every episode.

## 12. Provenance chain
RawObservation
  captured_by: app/version

EvaluationOutcome
  produced_by: evaluator/version

MemoryEvidence
  produced_by: evidence mapper/version

LearnerState
  produced_by: memory model/version
The system must be able to trace derived conclusions back toward historical observations. Provenance may be minimal in MVP but the model must preserve the boundary.

## 13. Historical reinterpretation
Mneme evolution adds interpretations rather than rewriting history.
RawObservation O1
   ├─ EvaluationOutcome v1
   │      └─ MemoryEvidence v1
   └─ EvaluationOutcome v2 (later, optional)
          └─ MemoryEvidence v2 (later, optional)
- Original observation remains unchanged.
- Historical outcome/evidence remains auditable.
- Later reinterpretation is marked as derived and identifies its producer/version.
- Current MemoryModel may choose the latest trusted evidence set without erasing previous interpretations.

## 14. Corrections
Normal history is append-only. If the learner or app must correct accidental input, a correction record references the original event rather than mutating it in place.
correction_event:
  target_event_id: "lev_..."
  reason: accidental_rating
  replacement_semantics:
    rating: good

## 15. Diagnostics
Diagnostics are derived analytical views over history and current learner-model outputs. They are never source-of-truth historical records.

### 15.1 Initial MVP/near-term diagnostics
- Frequently weak or repeatedly failed KnowledgePoints.
- Repeated hint dependence.
- Recent exposure contamination that makes a recall result less trustworthy.
- Long response latency combined with otherwise successful recall.
- Mismatch between self-assessment and deterministic evaluator outcome where both exist.
- RecallForm-specific weakness or repeated failure of one presentation direction.
- Potential leech/pathology: repeated lapses suggesting poor content, interference, missing prerequisite, or need for redesign.

### 15.2 Diagnostic uncertainty
Diagnostics must preserve uncertainty. Where attribution is ambiguous or evidence coverage is low, the system should report insufficient evidence rather than invent a precise diagnosis.

## 16. Learner-state boundary
Learning history
    ↓
Evaluation / Evidence
    ↓
MemoryModel
    ↓
model-specific LearnerState
    ↓
Diagnostics / Scheduling inputs
- LearnerState is latent, derived, rebuildable, and model-specific.
- Unknown is semantically different from weak/forgotten.
- No fixed universal mastery scalar is required.
- Any cached learner state may be discarded and recomputed from durable history.
- Model-specific confidence/uncertainty is optional; absence of it must not be replaced by fake precision.

## 17. Durability hierarchy

| Layer | Durability | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge + provenance | Durable | Versioned authored history and references must remain interpretable. |
| LearningEpisode/Event + RawObservation | Highest learner-data protection | Irreplaceable account of what happened. |
| EvaluationOutcome | High | Historical evaluator conclusion; may coexist with later reevaluation. |
| MemoryEvidence | High but interpretive | Versioned attribution; may coexist with reinterpretations. |
| LearnerState / diagnostics | Rebuildable | Cache/materialized inference, not historical truth. |
| Schedule cache | Disposable/rebuildable | Can be recomputed from state + policy. |


## 18. Privacy and retention
- Raw responses may contain sensitive personal, school, work, or professional information.
- The app should collect only observations with plausible learning/reinterpretation value.
- Heavy audio/video/raw artifacts should not be retained by default merely because the schema permits them.
- Backup and export must preserve user control over learner history.
- External analytics/export adapters are future work and must not silently transmit private learning history.

## 19. Schema evolution
- LearningEpisode/Event/Observation/Evidence schemas have explicit schema versions.
- Old stored records are decoded into current normalized domain semantics; they are not repeatedly rewritten.
- Additive fields remain absent/unknown in old data.
- Semantic meaning of an existing persisted field must not silently change.
- App writes current schema and supports a declared range of historical schema versions.
- Restore validates schema/version support before any destructive write.

## 20. Flashcard MVP behavior
The current successful flashcard UX stays intentionally simple while producing Mneme-compatible history.
Prompt shown
   ↓
Learner attempts recall
   ↓
Reveal answer
   ↓
Again / Hard / Good / Easy
   ↓
LearningEpisode completed
- No extra confidence question is required.
- Do not ask the learner to classify error cause.
- Automatically capture available timing and exposure metadata.
- For current self-assessed cards, minimal EvaluationOutcome and MemoryEvidence are sufficient.
- Future typed-answer or structured RecallForms can add deterministic evaluator outcomes without changing the episode/history architecture.

## 21. Legacy migration
Existing historical card reviews may be migrated conservatively into minimal Mneme episodes/events. Missing historical information must remain unknown.
legacy card review
   ↓
LearningEpisode
  Attempt/Rating observation
  minimal historical evaluation
  minimal evidence

Unavailable old fields:
  hint history = unknown
  raw answer = unknown
  exact prompt snapshot = unknown if not reconstructable

| Migration rule: Never fabricate high-resolution evidence from coarse legacy history. |
|---|


## 22. Implementation sequence
- Introduce LearningEpisode + ordered LearningEvent persistence for the current front/back review flow.
- Capture minimal RawObservation: rating, response timing, RecallForm/version, actual reveal/exposure context.
- Produce minimal historical EvaluationOutcome for self-assessed reviews.
- Produce minimal versioned MemoryEvidence for declared KnowledgePoint targets when justified.
- Add immutable execution snapshot/provenance references.
- Wire Requirement 02 state reducer/scheduler to consume evidence rather than mutate history.
- Add legacy review-history migration with unknown-preserving semantics.
- Add rebuild/integrity tests: delete derived state and reconstruct it from history.
- Add richer evaluator/outcome types only when new RecallForms require them.

## 23. Acceptance criteria
- Every completed MVP review can be represented as a LearningEpisode with deterministically ordered event semantics.
- RawObservation is distinguishable from EvaluationOutcome and MemoryEvidence in the domain model.
- Answer reveal/exposure is not recorded as successful retrieval.
- RecallForm target coverage does not automatically create evidence for every target.
- Missing/ambiguous evidence remains unknown or insufficient rather than false/incorrect.
- Historical evaluator/evidence provenance and artifact versions are retained sufficiently for interpretation.
- Knowledge updates/import cannot overwrite learner history.
- Derived learner state and diagnostics can be rebuilt without rewriting historical episodes/events.
- Legacy review migration does not fabricate unavailable raw responses, hints, or detailed attribution.
- Current front/back + Again/Hard/Good/Easy UX remains usable without additional required learner prompts.

## 24. Non-goals for MVP
- Learned/AI EvidenceMapper as a required component.
- Full multi-step tutoring engine.
- Research-grade model-run experiment infrastructure.
- Universal competence or understanding diagnosis.
- Cross-device CRDT/event merging semantics.
- xAPI/Caliper learning-record export.
- Automatic ontology split/merge based on diagnostics.

## 25. Relationship to Requirement 04
Requirement 03 defines semantic durability and evidence boundaries only. Requirement 04 will decide concrete local persistence shapes, indexes, transaction boundaries, append-only enforcement, cache/materialization strategy, backup mapping, and legacy DB migration. The database schema must implement these semantics without redefining them.

## 26. Final invariants
- Observation is not learner state.
- Evaluation is not memory attribution.
- Evidence is interpretation-relative and provenance-bearing.
- Historical observation is never rewritten to match a newer model.
- Unknown is not failure.
- Exposure is not retrieval.
- Derived state is replaceable; learner history is durable.
- The MVP simplifies behavior, not semantics.

## 27. Source-Code-Informed Evidence Amendments (v2.2)
These amendments strengthen capture durability, exposure provenance, reinterpretation, and scalable history inspection.

### 27.1 Raw capture durability barrier
The canonical local RawObservation/LearningEvent transaction must commit before optional compression, embedding, AI evaluation, remote sync, or diagnostics. Optional processing may be retried without duplicating the learner interaction.

### 27.2 Information origin / system exposure context
Historical evidence interpretation must preserve the relevant information the system exposed before an attempt: prompt content/version, hints, answer reveal, sibling/related RecallForms, worked examples or other assistance. The system records observable exposure provenance; it does not claim to know the learner’s internal cognitive source.

### 27.3 No semantic deduplication of learner history
Two similar learner responses at different times are two observations and may carry critical spacing/context information. They must never be consolidated as duplicates. Only retry of the same durable operation identity may collapse to one committed interaction.

### 27.4 Interpretation overlays
A later evaluator or EvidenceMapper may create a new interpretation of an old RawObservation. It appends a new versioned interpretation/supersession relationship rather than mutating the canonical observation or erasing the historical interpretation used at the time.

### 27.5 Progressive diagnostic reads
Diagnostics should retrieve history progressively (index/aggregate candidate → surrounding episode/timeline context → selected full observations) and may use structured/lexical/semantic discovery. Retrieval indexes remain rebuildable and cannot determine learner truth.

## 28. Dual-Profile Evidence Boundary (v2.3)
RawObservation/EvaluationOutcome/MemoryEvidence in this document are Learning Profile terms. Agent Memory also preserves source observations and provenance, but it does not automatically interpret them as evidence about a human learner or feed them into LearnerMemoryModel state.
- Information-origin/context-fencing is shared in spirit: system-provided context must remain distinguishable from independently observed information.
- Learning-history repetition is evidence and must not be semantically deduplicated. Agent Memory may consolidate derived records while retaining source observation lineage.
- Model-validation feedback about an evaluator/diagnostic is meta-evidence about the model, not learner MemoryEvidence and not Agent Memory truth.
- If shared storage is used, profile/type boundaries must remain explicit in serialization and replay.
