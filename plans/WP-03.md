## Agreed contract (current behaviour)

The agreed contract is docs `b514c16d` / code `619540ae`, approved by Codex in `ea4abfd2`.

- **Arguments.** `project_history(reader, stream, relations)`; the stream is
  `{store_id, entries[{position, record}]}` with positions strictly increasing from 1.
- **Order.** Generic stages; reader, stream and **early** lineage shape checks; entries in position
  order through the Layer 02 gates; **late** lineage graph checks; resolution.
- **Atomic failure (G-070).** The first failing entry fails the projection, with its `position`.
  For `history.unknown_required` the detail gives the full routing triple and no argument is
  named.
- **Repeated record IDs (G-073).** An identical canonical envelope **coalesces** and is reported in
  `duplicates`; different content is `history.duplicate_record_id` at that position.
- **Relations (G-071, G-072, G-075).**
  - The vocabulary is `supersedes`, `corrects`, `reinterprets`, `derived_from`.
  - Targets are typed: `to_record_id`, or, for `derived_from` only, `to_artifact` as
    `{stable_id, artifact_version}` or `{content_hash}`.
  - Equal relations **collapse** to one.
  - No self-links. The resolution family must be acyclic, and so must `derived_from` on its own.
  - Resolution-family endpoints must be available; unavailable `derived_from` targets are kept as
    `unresolved_derivations`.
- **Resolution (G-069, G-074).** Current heads are set-valued, with no tie-break. Interpretations
  come from direct `reinterprets` links only.
- **Result.** `through`, `records`, `skipped`, `excluded`, `duplicates`, `relations`,
  `unresolved_derivations`, `current`, `interpretations`.
- **Corpus.** `core.projection.canonical-path` (18 steps) and
  `core.projection.lineage-current-heads` (36 steps): **54 ACTIVE steps**.
- **Feasibility.** `assurance/layers/03-feasibility-matrix.md`, complete per member, with evidence.
